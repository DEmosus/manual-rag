# Surface quality and gas cutting

Related: [Welder and procedure qualification](welder-qualifications.md) | [Scope, Standard/Limit ranges and materials](materials-and-standards.md) | [Index](index.md)

## Definitions of surface conditions
- Minor imperfections are pitting, rolled-in scale, indentations, roll marks, scratches and grooves. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 4.2.1, PDF page 6.)
- Defects are cracks, shells, sand patches, sharp edged seams and minor imperfections exceeding the limits of table 1. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 4.2.1, PDF page 6.)
- For depth of imperfections or defects: the depth is to be measured from the surface of the product. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 4.2.1, PDF page 6.)

## Acceptance without remedies
- Minor imperfections, in accordance with the nominal thickness (t) of the product and the limits described in Table 1, are permissible and may be left as they are. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 4.2.2, PDF page 6.)

Table 1 - Limits for depth of minor imperfection, for acceptance without remedies. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 4.2.2, Table 1, PDF page 6.)

| Imperfection surface area Ratio (%) | 15-20% | 5-15% | 0-5% |
|---|---|---|---|
| t < 20 mm | 0.2 mm | 0.4 mm | 0.5 mm |
| 20 mm ≤ t < 50 mm | 0.2 mm | 0.6 mm | 0.7 mm |
| 50 mm ≤ t | 0.2 mm | 0.7 mm | 0.9 mm |

- Imperfection surface area Ratio (%) is obtained as influenced area / area under consideration (i.e. plate surface area) x 100%. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 4.2.2, PDF page 7.)
- For isolated surface discontinuities, influenced area is obtained by drawing a continuous line which follows the circumference of the discontinuity at a distance of 20 mm (Figure 1). (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 4.2.2, PDF page 7.)
- For surface discontinuities appearing in a cluster, influenced area is obtained by drawing a continuous line which follows the circumference of the cluster at a distance of 20 mm (Figure 2). (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 4.2.2, PDF page 7.)

## Remedial of defects and further defects
- Defects are to be remedied by grinding and/or welding in accordance with IACS UR W11 (ref. A6). (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 4.2.3, PDF page 8.)
- Lamination: investigation is to be carried out at the steelmill into the cause and extent of the detected laminations. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 4.2.4.1, PDF page 8.)
- Severe lamination is to be remedied by local insert plates; the minimum breadth or length of the plate to be replaced is 1600 mm for shell and strength deck plating in way of cruciform or T-joints, 800 mm for shell, strength deck plating and other primary members, and 300 mm for other structural members. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 4.2.4.1, PDF page 8.)
- Local limited lamination may be remedied by chipping and/or grinding followed by welding in accordance with sketch (a); where it is near the plate surface, the remedial may be carried out as shown in sketch (b). For limitations see paragraph 4.2.2. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 4.2.4.1, PDF page 8.)
- Loose weld spatters are to be removed by grinding or other measures to clean metal surface (see Table 9.13), as required by the paint system, on shell plating, deck plating on exposed decks, in tanks for chemical cargoes, in tanks for fresh water and for drinking water, and in tanks for lubricating oil, hydraulic oil, including service tanks. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 4.2.4.2, PDF page 8.)

## Gas cutting
- The roughness of the cut edges is to meet the following requirements, given as Standard and Limit values for Free Edges and Welding Edges. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 5, PDF page 8.)

Roughness of gas-cut free edges. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 5, PDF page 8.)

| Free Edges | Standard | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Strength Members | 150 µm | 300 µm |
| Others | 500 µm | 1000 µm |

Roughness of gas-cut welding edges. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 5, PDF page 8.)

| Welding Edges | Standard | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Strength Members | 400 µm | 800 µm |
| Others | 800 µm | 1500 µm |
