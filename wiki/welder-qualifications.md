# Welder and procedure qualification

Related: [Scope, Standard/Limit ranges and materials](materials-and-standards.md) | [Surface quality and gas cutting](surface-quality-and-cutting.md) | [Index](index.md)

## General requirements for new construction
- In general, the work is to be carried out in accordance with the Classification Society rules and under the supervision of the Surveyor to the Classification Society. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 2.1, PDF page 4.)
- Welding operations are to be carried out in accordance with work instructions accepted by the Classification Society. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 2.2, PDF page 5.)
- Welding of hull structures is to be carried out by qualified welders, according to approved and qualified welding procedures and with welding consumables approved by the Classification Society, see Section 3. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 2.3, PDF page 5.)
- Welding operations are to be carried out under proper supervision by the shipbuilder. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 2.3, PDF page 5.)
- The working conditions for welding are to be monitored by the Classification Society in accordance with UR Z23 (ref. A12). (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 2.3, PDF page 5.)

## Qualification of welders
- Welders are to be qualified in accordance with UR W32 (ref. A18) or other recognized standard accepted by the Classification Society. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 3.1.1, PDF page 5.)
- Recognition of other standards is subject to submission to the Classification Society for evaluation. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 3.1.1, PDF page 5.)
- Subcontractors are to keep records of welders qualification and, when required, furnish valid approval test certificates. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 3.1.1, PDF page 5.)
- Welding operators using fully mechanized or fully automatic processes need generally not pass approval testing provided that the production welds made by the operators are of the required quality. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 3.1.2, PDF page 5.)
- However, operators are to receive adequate training in setting or programming and operating the equipment. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 3.1.2, PDF page 5.)
- Records of training and operation experience shall be maintained on individual operator’s files and records, and be made available to the Classification Society for inspection when requested. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 3.1.2, PDF page 5.)

## Qualification of welding procedures and NDT operators
- Welding procedures are to be qualified in accordance with UR W28 (ref. A10) or other recognized standard accepted by the Classification Society. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 3.2, PDF page 5.)
- Personnel performing non-destructive testing for the purpose of assessing quality of welds in connection with new construction covered by this standard, are to be qualified in accordance with Classification Society rules or to a recognized international or national qualification scheme. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 3.3, PDF page 5.)
- Records of operators and their current certificates are to be kept and made available to the Surveyor for inspection. (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 3.3, PDF page 5.)
- In case of non-destructive examination carried out by an independent firm from the shipbuilder, such firm has to comply with UR W35 (Ref. A20). (Source: IACS Rec. 47 Part A, Sec. 3.3, PDF page 5.)
